# WorkTrust International - Modern React Digital Banking Platform

A modern, full-featured digital banking platform built with **React**, **Vite**, and **React Router**. Faithfully maintaining the original design, assets, and branding of WorkTrust International with complete multi-page routing and direct WhatsApp order integration.

## Pages & Structure
- `/` - **Home**: Hero banner, features, process, solutions, pricing plans, testimonials, marquee, and CTA.
- `/about-us` - **About Us**: Company mission, vision, statistics counters, trust features.
- `/how-it-works` - **How It Works**: 4-step workflow, video showcase, and onboarding details.
- `/solutions` - **Solutions**: Financial services and **Interactive Loan & Payback Calculator**.
- `/portfolio` - **Portfolios & Plans**: High-yielding DPS/FDR schemes, loan tiers, and comparison table.
- `/rewards` - **Rewards & Referrals**: 4-tier VIP loyalty program (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) and 3-level referral commissions.
- `/blog` - **Blog**: Filterable & searchable articles directory.
- `/blog/:id` - **Blog Details**: Individual article reading view, comments, and related articles.
- `/contact` - **Contact Us**: Contact cards, WhatsApp direct chat, and direct message inquiry form.
- `/faq` - **FAQ**: Searchable & categorized question directory.
- `/privacy-policy` - **Privacy Policy**: Regulatory compliance & data protection guidelines.
- `/terms-and-conditions` - **Terms & Conditions**: Full legal terms of service.
- `/login` - **Account Login**: Authentication page with password toggle.
- `/register` - **Sign Up**: Multi-field account creation with country selection.

## Tech Stack
- **React 19**
- **Vite**
- **React Router 7**
- **Lucide Icons & FontAwesome Pro**
- **Swiper** for responsive carousels
